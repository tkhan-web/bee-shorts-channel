# Bee Shorts: Pollination Explained

## Short concept
Title: "Bees Do More Than Make Honey"
Length: 20-25 seconds
Format: YouTube Shorts, vertical 9:16
Style: Fast-paced visuals, bold text overlay, simple educational narration

## Shot-by-shot plan

### 0:00-0:02
Visual: Close-up of a bee landing on a flower.
Text overlay: "Bees do more than make honey"
Audio: quick upbeat intro sound

### 0:02-0:05
Visual: Bee moving across flower; pollen sticks to legs.
Text overlay: "They carry pollen"

### 0:05-0:08
Visual: Quick cuts between flowers with animated pollen dots transferring.
Text overlay: "From flower to flower"

### 0:08-0:11
Visual: Simple diagram or animation showing pollen traveling.
Text overlay: "This is called pollination"

### 0:11-0:15
Visual: Flower transforms into fruit or seed pod; quick montage of fruits and vegetables.
Text overlay: "It helps plants make fruits + seeds"

### 0:15-0:18
Visual: Cut to common foods: apple, tomato, berry, pumpkin.
Text overlay: "Without bees, many foods disappear"

### 0:18-0:21
Visual: Bee flying in slow motion, then fast movement as it works.
Text overlay: "Tiny workers. Huge impact."

### 0:21-0:24
Visual: Final wide shot of flower field or hive.
Text overlay: "Bees help grow your food"

## Optional voiceover script
"Bees do more than make honey. They carry pollen from flower to flower. This process is called pollination. Pollination helps plants make fruits and seeds. Without bees, many foods we eat would not grow. So next time you see a bee, remember: it is helping grow our food."

## Editing notes
- Use fast cuts every 1-2 seconds to keep it engaging.
- Keep text overlay large and readable in vertical format.
- Add subtle motion graphics for pollen particles.
- Use a crisp nature soundtrack or upbeat educational background music.
- End with a clean final frame and a call to action.
